Rescue Profiles

Transparent screening criteria for Water Rescue, Mountain or Wilderness Rescue, and Disaster Rescue or Individual Tracking.

Screening criteria

These criteria narrow records for human review. They do not replace health, temperament, drive, or field evaluation.

Water Rescue

17 current strict candidates.

  • Breeds: Labrador Retriever Mix, Chesapeake Bay Retriever, Newfoundland
  • Sex: Intact Female
  • Age: 26–156 weeks

Mountain or Wilderness Rescue

5 current strict candidates.

  • Breeds include German Shepherd, Alaskan Malamute, Old English Sheepdog, Siberian Husky, and Rottweiler
  • Sex: Intact Male
  • Age: 26–156 weeks

Disaster Rescue or Individual Tracking

4 current strict candidates.

  • Breeds include Doberman Pinscher, German Shepherd, Golden Retriever, Bloodhound, and Rottweiler
  • Sex: Intact Male
  • Age: 20–300 weeks

How matching works

The matcher compares each shelter record's normalized breed, recorded sex, and raw age-in-weeks against the selected profile. The original shelter values remain available for review.

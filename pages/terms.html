Terms of Use

Rescue Retriever is a portfolio and educational demonstration of preliminary shelter-dog search-and-rescue screening. It does not provide live shelter availability, veterinary advice, rescue-team certification, adoption placement, or a guarantee that an animal is suitable for training.

Use and limitations

You may browse the demo and export candidate information for learning or evaluation. Its matching rules are intentionally limited to the available source attributes. Make independent, current inquiries with shelters and qualified professionals before acting on any record.
